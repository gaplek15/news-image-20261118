## The day in brief

- **APEC opens in Shenzhen:** the summit got underway as the QUAD announced a new plan in Manila, where Trump arrived — and the first-ever Trump–Xi–Putin trilateral is set for tomorrow, 19 November, in Shenzhen.
- **Kerch bridge battered:** Ukraine heavily damaged the Crimea bridge for a second day running, while Russia pounded bridges near Kyiv.
- **Gulf tensions:** Iran struck an American radar site in Bahrain, and Kuwait intercepted a drone.
- **Nvidia's huge quarter:** revenue hit $115.84 billion, double the year before; the board said it will not restore Jensen Huang as CEO, while Trump backed Huang's return.
- **Dhoni bows out:** MS Dhoni retired from the IPL and will mentor Chennai Super Kings.
- **Storm coming:** Cyclone Akvan headed for Andhra Pradesh, expected to make landfall around 20 November.
- **Trade and sanctions:** Canada and China extended their trade deal as Xi met Carney, and Britain sanctioned companies behind Israeli settlements.

## How it works

`main.py` copies the bundled front page (`news.png`, 1024×1024) to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png` without changing a single byte. No network, no clock, no randomness — fully deterministic. Run it twice and the two outputs are byte-identical.
